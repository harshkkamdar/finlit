## CHAT 1: CHIP (Style Lock + Mascot)

Start here. Chip establishes the visual style that all other characters must match.

### Prompt:

I'm building character illustrations for a financial literacy app called FinoLingo. I need you to establish and lock in a visual style with this first character, then I'll come back for 4 more human characters in the same style.

**Art style rules (follow these EXACTLY):**
- Flat vector illustration, clean confident outlines (2-3px stroke weight)
- NOT anime, NOT Pixar/3D, NOT corporate clipart, NOT emoji-style
- Think: Headspace app illustrations crossed with Notion avatars crossed with Kurzgesagt
- Minimal detail in clothing folds/texture, keep shapes clean and readable at small sizes (40px)
- Consistent 2px rounded outlines on all shapes, slightly darker than the fill color
- Subtle shadows only (no harsh drop shadows), use a slightly darker shade of the base color at ~10% opacity
- Transparent/white background, NO environment or scenery

**Color palette (ONLY these):**
- Primary Green: #1B6B4A
- Accent Orange/Amber: #F5A623
- Dark Navy: #1A1A2E (outlines, dark elements)
- Warm Off-White: #FAFAF8 (highlights, eye whites)
- Muted Gray: #596673

**Character: CHIP (App Mascot)**

Chip is a sentient ₹10 rupee coin that came to life. NOT a robot, NOT a generic smiley coin. Think of a real Indian coin that grew a face, tiny stubby arms, and short legs.

Design details:
- Body is a coin (circle) with warm metallic gold surface (#F5A623 base, subtle #1B6B4A embossed ₹ symbol on its belly)
- Ridged/reeded edge visible on the sides (like a real coin edge)
- Large expressive eyes (oval, #FAFAF8 whites, #1A1A2E pupils) that do most of the emotional work
- Small curved mouth that changes dramatically per expression
- Tiny stubby arms (rounded mitt hands, no fingers) and short legs extending from coin edge
- A tiny crack/chip on one edge (that's where the name comes from, makes it endearing)
- Vibe: wise beyond its years, genuinely funny, a little sarcastic

**Generate these 7 expressions as SEPARATE images, 512x512 minimum:**

1. **chip-neutral** — Friendly resting face, slight smile, eyes forward, one hand on hip. "I'm here to help" energy.

2. **chip-excited** — Big open-mouth smile, eyes squeezed into happy crescents, both arms raised in celebration. Tiny sparkle marks around it. "You got it right!" energy.

3. **chip-thinking** — Hand on chin, eyes looking up and to the side, mouth a small squiggle. "Hmm, let me think..." energy.

4. **chip-shocked** — Eyes wide as saucers, mouth a perfect O, arms thrown out to sides. Tiny sweat drop. "Wait, WHAT?!" energy.

5. **chip-warning** — Serious expression, eyebrows angled down, one hand up in "stop" gesture, slight frown. "Careful now..." energy.

6. **chip-sarcastic** — Half-lidded eyes (one eyebrow raised higher), smirk on one side, arms crossed. "Oh really?" energy.

7. **chip-proud** — Chest puffed out (coin body subtly inflated), eyes closed with satisfaction, big smile, hands on hips power pose. "That's my student!" energy.
